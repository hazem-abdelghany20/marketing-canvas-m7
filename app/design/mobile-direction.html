Marketing Canvas · mobile · direction 2

Trace home, one row per layer

B's bottom bar with Chat in the middle, B's trace as the home screen, and C's board and dock when you switch to Board. The phone is wired to the seeded board, so swiping works: use a trackpad or touch on the rows, or tap a dot or a card that's peeking in.

How Trace works

  • Each layer is a row: Goal → Strategy → Campaign → Content. Swipe a row to move along it.
  • A row only holds what serves the card that's on screen in the row above it. Swiping Vayn → Mental Diet redraws every row beneath.
  • Changing a row resets the rows below it to their first card.
  • Tap the card that's on screen to open its detail sheet. Tap a card that's peeking in to bring it on screen.
  • Find sends you back here: tap a piece of content and Trace lines up its whole chain.
  • A layer with nothing in it shows "Nothing serves this yet", with the Add action for that type.

Decisions I made — say if you want them different

  • Fifth tab is Add. Chat in the middle needs an odd number of tabs. Add opens the add-node menu, so I took + out of the board dock to avoid having it twice.
  • Which goal opens first: the one you last looked at, falling back to the first goal on the board.
  • Assets, notes and comments are not rows. They appear as chips on the card they relate to, so the four rows stay a clean serves chain.
  • Nodes that serve nothing (e.g. "IG reach down 30%") can't appear in a goal's tree, so Find lists them under "Not connected".
