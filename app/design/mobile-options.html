Three ways the app could work on a phone

Same tokens, same type and the same seeded board as the desktop app. The options differ in what the phone opens to and where the canvas, the whiteboard tools and the assistant sit. Tap the states under each phone, or tap inside it.

Option A

Canvas first

The desktop app made smaller. The board fills the screen, the dock becomes a tray at the bottom, and chat and node detail come up as sheets over the board.

Option B

Trace first

Phones are for reading the graph, not drawing it. You open to a node and see its serves chain up to the goal, one tap per step. The board is a tab you switch to when you need it.

Works because

  • It is the spec's own idea, pointing at a piece of content and tracing it back to why it exists, as the first thing you see.
  • Lists and taps instead of pinch-and-drag, so it stays usable one-handed.
  • "No goal" shows up in Find as a gap you can fix.

Costs

  • The whiteboard is behind a tab and a "Mark up" button, so drawing on a phone becomes secondary.
Option C

Assistant first

On a phone you type more than you drag. You open to chat, with a live strip of the board above it. Citations light up the strip, proposals land on it, and one tap takes you to the full board.
